Privacy Policy

For the "gws" Google Workspace command-line app · Last updated 13 October 2026

Who this app is for

"gws" is a personal command-line tool run by Frederick Kankam on his own computer to manage his own Google accounts (Calendar, Gmail and Drive). It is not offered to the public.

What data it accesses

  • Google Calendar events, to read and organise the owner's schedule.
  • Gmail and Google Drive content, only when the owner asks for it.

How data is used and stored

Data is used only to carry out the owner's own requests. OAuth tokens are stored encrypted on the owner's computer. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account → Security → Third-party access.
